# Market Regimes

Track how setups behave in different environments.

## Regime taxonomy

- TREND_UP
- TREND_DOWN
- RANGE
- TRANSITION
- HIGH_VOLATILITY
- LOW_VOLATILITY
- EVENT_DRIVEN
- DATA_UNCERTAIN

## Record

```text
DATE:
SYMBOL:
REGIME:
SETUP:
OUTCOME:
R:
NOTES:
```

## Research objective

Over time estimate which setup families perform under which regimes.

Do not infer a reliable edge from a tiny sample.
