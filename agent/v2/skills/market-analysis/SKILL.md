# Market Analysis

## Purpose

Perform deep analysis of a candidate and answer:

> Is there a defined directional edge with a clear invalidation and acceptable risk/reward?

## Multi-timeframe process

Use, where data permits:

- Higher timeframe: regime and major structure.
- Middle timeframe: setup formation.
- Lower timeframe: entry refinement.

Do not use a lower timeframe signal to override a major higher-timeframe invalidation without explicitly explaining the conflict.

## Analysis

### 1. Regime
Classify:

- bullish trend
- bearish trend
- range
- transition
- high-volatility / unstable
- insufficient data

### 2. Structure
Identify:

- swing highs/lows
- BOS
- CHoCH
- support/resistance
- range boundaries
- liquidity pools
- invalidation levels

### 3. Price action
Check:

- displacement
- rejection
- breakout quality
- retest
- failed breakout
- wick behavior
- candle close relative to key level

### 4. Volume
Check whether volume confirms or contradicts the move.

### 5. Derivatives
Evaluate:

- OI rising/falling relative to price;
- funding direction and extremity;
- liquidations;
- basis;
- positioning imbalance.

Never interpret one derivatives metric in isolation.

### 6. Thesis challenge

After forming a preliminary thesis, search specifically for reasons it is wrong.

Required questions:

- What would make the opposite direction more likely?
- Is the move already overextended?
- Is the breakout likely to be a liquidity grab?
- Is OI behavior inconsistent with the expected continuation?
- Is there nearby opposing liquidity that destroys the R:R?
- Is there an event risk invalidating the setup?

## Evidence classification

Mark important conclusions as:

`CONFIRMED`, `STRONG_INFERENCE`, `UNCONFIRMED`, `CONTRADICTED`.
